# 15 — Product Pivot Training

Weak idea → force specificity:

1. Specific user
2. Specific pain (freq + severity)
3. Specific workflow (steps before/after)
4. Unique data/context
5. Differentiated AI capability (pattern)
6. Compelling demo moment

Example seed: "Generic AI study assistant"  
Keep pivoting until a judge could film a 60s spot.

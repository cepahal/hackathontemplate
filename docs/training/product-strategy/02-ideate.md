# 02 — Generate Ideas (15–20, diverse)

## WHAT
Explore **categories**, not 20 flavors of chatbot.

## Categories to cover
consumer · enterprise · developer tools · healthcare · education · finance · productivity · social · infrastructure · AI agents · computer vision · automation · data products

## Card format (every idea)
```text
Problem:
User:
Pain:
Current workaround:
Product:
AI role:          # pattern from 04 — or "none / weak → kill"
Technical approach:
Differentiation:
Demo:             # 1 memorable moment
Risks:
```

## Rules
- No superficial renames ("ChatGPT but for students" without workflow)
- At least 3 non-chat UI ideas (vision, automation, data product, infra)
- Mark decorative-AI ideas for kill

## TIME BOX
10–15 minutes for volume. Validation comes next — don't polish yet.

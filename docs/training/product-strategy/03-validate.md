# 03 — Problem Validation

For each serious idea:

### User
Exact persona (role + context), not "everyone."

### Pain
Frequency: daily / weekly / event-driven?  
Severity: time, money, risk, embarrassment, compliance?

### Existing solution
Spreadsheet, email, incumbent SaaS, do-nothing, ChatGPT paste.

### Gap
Why insufficient: slow, error-prone, no context, no audit, no integration, no mobile, etc.

### AI advantage
What becomes newly possible: perception, generation under constraints, extraction, planning over tools, personalization with **private** context.

## Kill test
If removing AI leaves an equally good product → AI was decorative → kill or redesign.
